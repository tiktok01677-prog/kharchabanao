Privacy policy

Personal budgeting should stay personal.

Effective date: November 12, 2026

KharchaBanao is a local-first personal finance journal. The provided website does not use accounts, a developer database, advertising SDKs or analytics tracking.

Information you enter

You may enter transaction descriptions, dates, amounts, categories, notes, budget limits, savings targets and monthly reminders. You can also set a wallet name, currency display and opening balance.

Where data is stored

These records are stored locally in your browser's or Android WebView's localStorage, associated with the website origin. The provided app does not automatically transmit your finance records to a developer server or sync them across devices. No bank or payment account is connected.

How the information is used

Saved entries drive your on-device expense calendar, budget comparisons, charts, savings progress and recurring schedule list. Monthly reminders are recorded only when you explicitly choose to do so.

Backups, exports and sharing

You can download a JSON backup containing your records or export transactions as CSV. Those files may contain sensitive personal information. Files you save, email, share or upload are under your control and are not protected by the app after export.

Hosting and technical information

If this website is published on GitHub Pages or a different host, the hosting provider may process standard network information such as IP addresses and request logs. This policy does not cover independent processing by the hosting provider, browser or Android system. Review the host's own privacy policy.

Storage security

LocalStorage is not an encrypted vault. People with access to your unlocked phone, computer, browser profile or exported files may be able to read your records. Avoid entering banking passwords, card credentials or other secrets.

Data retention and deletion

Records remain in localStorage until you delete them using Settings → Delete all local data, clear browser/WebView storage, or remove the app's stored site data. Exported backups must be deleted separately. Clearing storage or uninstalling the app may permanently delete records if you have no backup.

Children

KharchaBanao is intended for general personal finance organization, not specifically directed toward children.

Changes

App or privacy practice changes may require policy updates. The effective date will be revised when appropriate.
