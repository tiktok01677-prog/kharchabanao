About the service

KharchaBanao provides personal expense logging, budgeting, savings planning and monthly scheduling features for informational purposes. It is not a bank, payment processor, regulated financial advisor or accounting service.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for entering accurate information, protecting your device and backups and using the app lawfully. Balances and reports only reflect entries you record; they do not verify bank balances.

No automatic payments

Recurring schedules are reminders inside the app. They do not initiate transfers, send notifications or automatically add transactions.

Availability

Data depends on the local browser/WebView storage. Browser data removal, device problems or software changes may erase local records. Make regular JSON backups.

No financial advice

Charts and budgets are planning aids, not personalized investment, tax, legal or financial advice.

Changes and contact

Features and policies may change. Website owners should add their real contact details on the Contact page before public distribution.
